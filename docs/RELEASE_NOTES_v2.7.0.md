## SAXS/WAXS — SAXS 2.0 (new)

Three new tabs in the SAXS/WAXS workspace, seven new core modules and a headless pipeline. Everything is sample-agnostic: grouping, ordering, colours, markers and x-values come from a series table you supply; nothing in the code knows a sample name.

- **Frames tab**: inventory of raw 2D frames (EDF) from a beamstopless instrument — role from regexes (sample / empty / calibrant), session, configuration, the beam centre refined from the recorded direct beam (header centres were off by 0.5–0.8 mm in three of five sessions of a real campaign), direct-beam level, saturation, and the lobe / streak diagnostics that expose the capillary-wall light on the horizontal detector axis (the artefact that averages into a fake low-q maximum). Samples are paired with the empty of the same folder, configuration and capillary (`same_folder`, `nearest_time` or explicit rules). **Reduce all pairs** integrates the four diagonal wedges only, masks the bar zone, takes the transmission from the beam footprint with a central-disc uniformity check, classifies every loading (measurable / marginal / opaque / unreliable), flags the usable q-range and normalises per mm of material from a samples table (μ per sample, or composition + density through xraydb at the frame's energy). One `.dat` per sample with the full provenance in its header; the curves join the workspace.
- **Unified fit tab**: the two-level unified model (Beaucage 1995; Tomita et al. 2024 Eq. 10) — a grain level with a free Porod term, an optional nanoscale level whose Porod prefactor is tied to its Guinier amplitude by the Beaucage 1996 relation (a level is a population with a knee or it is not kept), a flat background and an optional rising tail — with forward selection on the BIC (a term is added only when it lowers the BIC by more than 10), at-bound flags on every parameter, an amplitude floor against fake levels, and a **robustness pass** with relaxed grain bounds ("resolved" = kept by both with Rg within 20 %). Single fits with components and residuals, batch over selected curves, CSV / XLSX / JSON.
- **Series figures tab**: a series table (import / edit) drives the grid of normalised, shifted curves with labels at the curve ends, letters and family labels; fit-panel figures with residual panels; trends against your x-value (open markers = not quantified, faded = level not resolved, error bars when resolved); the 2D frame figure (crops about the refined centre + horizontal / vertical profiles). Export pdf / svg / png at any dpi. Tables as xlsx (fits, model-free, robustness, notes; frozen header, column widths), csv (utf-8 with BOM, ASCII-only cells so Excel never garbles a dash) and markdown.
- **Model-free descriptors** (`saxs.core.descriptors`): local log-log slopes, low-q exponent and Porod constant, high-q plateau and its ratio to the scaled empty, excess invariant over the grain baseline, Porod-plot shoulders, Kratky maximum, and an explicit maximum test in I, I q² and I q⁴ whose significance is checked against the propagated noise and the data's own scatter (a maximum of I is what a characteristic spacing produces; a maximum of I q² alone is the Guinier knee of a level).
- **Composite fit** gains two shared components, `beaucage_tied` and `rising_tail`, and the presets L1, L1_T, L1_L2, L1_L2_T; the Beaucage form is evaluated identically in both engines.
- **Headless pipeline**: `py -3.11 scripts/saxs_pipeline.py init project.json`, then `run project.json [--steps …]` runs inventory → reduce → describe → fit → robustness → figures → tables from one JSON file, each step reading the previous step's files (any step reruns alone), with a `manifest.json` recording code version, options, inputs, outputs and timings.
- Validation: synthetic frames and curves (Poisson noise anchored to the feature under test), selection and bound properties, UI wiring tests, and a real-data regression (env-gated: `PRISM_SAXS_REFERENCE_DIR`, `PRISM_SAXS_REDUCED_V2_DIR`) that reproduces a hand-made 43-frame campaign — pairing identical, reduced curves identical to the last digit, descriptors identical, all 39 two-level fits and their robustness flags identical from the same input.

## Fixes and internals

- `core/qt_worker.run_in_thread` accepts `on_progress`: the job receives a `progress(i, n, message)` callable whose calls reach the UI thread.
- The 1D loader recognises `I_corr` / `sigma_I` columns; `edf_sectors` gains `sector_anisotropy`, exposes the usable-range and centre options and tolerates frames without a wavelength header (virtual-detector exports) when a default is given.
